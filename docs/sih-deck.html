Why CivicPulse wins

Four things nobody else has together.

Fraud-proof by construction

No gallery bypass exists. We attacked our own database with the public key — forging points and reading other users' data were both refused.

Zero licence cost

No maps API, no SMS gateway, no AI billing, no paid hosting. A campus can adopt it without a budget approval.

Absolute transparency

Before-and-after photo proof on every closure, and a written reason on every rejection. The audit trail is public.

Built for low-end phones
